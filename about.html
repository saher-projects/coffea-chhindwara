Who We Are

The Heart Behind Every Cup

Coffea Chhindwara was born from a single dream — to create a space where every person walks in as a stranger and leaves as family. Nestled in the heart of Chhindwara at Diwedi Tower, we've crafted a sanctuary where the aroma of freshly roasted beans meets the warmth of genuine hospitality.

From handpicking only the finest Arabica and Robusta blends to training our baristas with Italian precision, every decision we make is guided by one principle: your happiness matters above everything else.
